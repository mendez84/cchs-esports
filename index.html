RESPECT THE GEAR

Lab Norms & Equipment Care

Our equipment is an investment in your program. Treat the room with pride.

🥪 Food, Drinks & Cleanliness

  • Outer Perimeter Eating: Lunch trays, snacks, and drinks must stay on outer tables away from PCs and Switch docks.
  • Clean Hands First: Wash hands before playing. Sanitizing wipes are provided at the entrance to wipe down controllers.
  • Clean Before the Bell: Discard all wrappers, bottles, and trash before leaving. Keep the lab cleaner than you found it.

🎮 Station & Hardware Etiquette

  • Gaming Chairs: Strictly for PC battle stations. Never drag computer chairs over to the Smash TV area.
  • Smash Station Rules: Winner stays, maximum 2 consecutive wins when other players are waiting in queue.
  • No Rage-Quitting: Zero tolerance for slamming controllers, headsets, or pulling cables. Report hardware issues directly to Coach Mendez.
PROGRAM VALUES

Coach Mendez’s 3 Pillars (2026–27)

Our foundation for building a competitive, welcoming, and sustainable program.

1

Everyone Stays Eligible

Minimum 2.0 GPA with no Fs. Grade checks happen prior to every match day. The goal isn't to bench players—it's to catch struggles early so we can tutor and recover together.

2

More Diversity & Welcoming Space

Reaching out beyond typical circles into art, theater, DATA Academy, and other clubs. Whether you're a competitive grinder or a casual gamer looking for AC at lunch, you belong here.

3

Leadership Beyond Playing

Real opportunities for student leadership: Game Captains, Stream Technicians, Video Editors, Shoutcasters, Graphic Designers, and Club Officers (President, VP, Secretary, Treasurer).

🔒

Student Privacy & District Safety Standards

Complies with FERPA (34 CFR Part 99), COPPA, California SOPIPA, and PSUSD Board Policies.
