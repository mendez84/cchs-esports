🛡️ Lab Rules & Equipment Norms

Our equipment is an investment in your program. Treat the room with pride so everyone can play.

🥪 Food, Drinks & Cleanliness

  • Lunch Trays & Snacks: Must be eaten on the outer tables away from keyboards, PCs, and Switch docks.
  • Clean Hands: Wash or sanitize hands before using controllers or peripherals (wipes are available at the entrance).
  • Clean Up: Throw away all wrappers, bottles, and trays before the bell rings. Leave the lab cleaner than you found it.

🎧 Gear & Station Etiquette

  • Gaming Chairs: Reserved strictly for PC battle stations. Do not drag them over to the Smash TV area.
  • Smash Stations: 3 TVs are set up for freeplay and crew rotations. Winner stays, max 2 consecutive matches when others are waiting.
  • Respect Equipment: No rage-quitting, slamming controllers, or pulling cords. Report any hardware bugs to Coach Mendez immediately.

📋 Coach Mendez's 3 Pillars

1

Everyone Stays Eligible

Minimum 2.0 GPA with no Fs. Grade checks happen before every match day. The goal isn't to kick anyone off—it's to catch struggles early so we can tutor and recover together.

2

More Diversity & Inclusion

Reaching out to students in art, theater, DATA, and beyond. Whether you're a competitive grinder or a casual gamer looking for AC at lunch, you belong here.

3

Grow Beyond Playing

Real opportunities in student leadership: Game Captains, Stream Production, Shoutcasting, Graphic Design, Video Editing, and Club Officers (President, VP, Secretary, Treasurer).

🔒 Student Privacy & District Compliance

FERPA & Student Records

No confidential student records, grades, or personal identifying numbers are published. Roster shoutouts are limited to gamer tags and require signed district media forms.
