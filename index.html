Lab Norms & Equipment Care

Our lab equipment is an investment in our students. Treat the space with pride so everyone can play.

🥪 Food, Drinks & Cleanliness

  • Perimeter Eating: Lunch trays, food, and drinks must be kept on the outer tables away from PCs, keyboards, and Switch docks.
  • Sanitize Hands: Wash or sanitize hands before touching equipment. Wipes are provided at the entrance to clean controllers after use.
  • Clean Before the Bell: Throw away all wrappers, bottles, and food before the bell rings. Leave the lab cleaner than you found it.

🎮 Station & Gear Etiquette

  • Gaming Chairs: Reserved strictly for PC battle stations. Do not drag gaming chairs over to the Smash TV area.
  • Smash Rotations: Winner stays, maximum 2 consecutive wins when other students are waiting for a turn.
  • Zero Equipment Abuse: No slamming controllers, pulling cords, or rage-quitting. Treat all hardware with care and report issues to Coach Mendez.

Coach Mendez’s 3 Pillars (2026–27)

Our guiding philosophy for Cathedral City High School esports athletes and staff.

1

Everyone Stays Eligible

Minimum 2.0 GPA with no Fs. Grade checks happen before every match day. The goal isn't to kick anyone off—it's to catch struggles early so we can tutor, support, and recover together.

2

More Diversity & Welcoming Space

Reaching out beyond typical circles into art, theater, DATA Academy, and other clubs. Whether you're a competitive grinder or a casual gamer looking for AC at lunch, you belong here.

3

Leadership Beyond Playing

Real opportunities for student leadership: Game Captains, Stream Technicians, Video Editors, Shoutcasters, Graphic Designers, and Club Officers (President, VP, Secretary, Treasurer).

Student Privacy, Safety & District Standards

Cathedral City High School Esports adheres to FERPA (34 CFR Part 99), COPPA, and California SOPIPA (Ed Code § 49073.1).

This website is a purely informational scholastic resource. It carries zero commercial tracking cookies, zero advertising pixels, and does not collect or sell student personal data. Player recognitions use approved gamer tags or require parent/guardian media release forms on file. Student interest forms and surveys are administered through district-authenticated accounts (@psusd.us) under PSUSD Board Policies 0410, 5131.2, and 6163.4.
