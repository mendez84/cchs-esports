📋 Standards & Eligibility

Esports athletes represent Cathedral City High School and are subject to the same student-athlete requirements as traditional sports.

🎓 Academic Standards

  • Must maintain a minimum 2.0 GPA with no failing grades (no Fs).
  • Bi-weekly grade checks are required to stay on active rosters.
  • Tutoring and academic assistance are available before match times.
  • Regular school attendance: unexcused absences on match days result in benching.

🤝 Code of Conduct & Sportsmanship

  • Strict zero-tolerance policy for toxicity, harassment, rage-quitting, or offensive language in-game or voice chat.
  • Adherence to PSUSD Student Code of Conduct at all times.
  • Respect coaches, teammates, opponents, and match officials. Win with humility, lose with class.
