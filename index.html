PROGRAM VALUES

More Than Just Gaming

CCHS Esports is built on the same foundation as any traditional varsity athletic program: discipline, communication, academic responsibility, and leadership.

🎯

Competitive Play

Compete in sanctioned high school leagues against rivals across Southern California and the nation.

📚

Academics First

Student-athletes are held to strict GPA and attendance standards. We support tutoring and study sessions before game time.

🤝

Community & Career

Gain real-world experience beyond playing: shoutcasting, video production, graphic design, social media, and team management.

TEAMS & TITLES

Competitive Titles

We field varsity, junior varsity, and club teams across major high school esports titles.

Console (Switch)

Super Smash Bros. Ultimate

1v1 and crew battle formats. Fast-paced platform fighter requiring precision, matchup knowledge, and split-second reactions.

Format: 1v1 / 3v3 Crew Day: Tuesdays
Cross-Platform

Rocket League

High-flying vehicular soccer. 3v3 squad play emphasizing aerial mechanics, rapid rotations, and non-stop communication.

Format: 3v3 Squad Day: Wednesdays
PC

Valorant

5v5 character-based tactical shooter. Coordinated team executes, utility usage, and clutch-round decision making.

Format: 5v5 Tactical Day: Thursdays
Expanding

Club & Additional Titles

League of Legends, Overwatch 2, Mario Kart, and fighting games depending on student interest and league offerings each semester.

Format: Team / Solo Day: Open Lab

STANDARDS

Player Eligibility & Expectations

To represent Cathedral City High School, all players must meet district athletic requirements.

📋 Academic Requirements

  • Maintain a minimum 2.0 GPA with no failing grades (no Fs).
  • Regular school attendance is mandatory—no unexcused absences on match days.
  • Complete mandatory academic grade checks throughout the season.

🎮 Conduct & Sportsmanship

  • Zero tolerance for toxicity, rage-quitting, or disrespectful behavior in-game or in voice chat.
  • Adhere to the PSUSD Student Code of Conduct at all times.
  • Respect teammates, opponents, coaches, and match officials (GGs win or lose).
