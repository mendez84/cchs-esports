Lab Norms & Equipment Etiquette

Our computers, consoles, and monitors are an investment in our students. Treat the room with pride.

  • Food & Trays: Lunch trays and snacks must stay on the perimeter tables away from keyboards, mice, and Switch docks.
  • Clean Hands: Wash hands before playing. Sanitizing wipes are available by the door—wipe down controllers when finished.
  • Chairs: Black gaming chairs stay at PC stations only. Do not drag them over to the Smash TVs.
  • Smash Rotations: Winner stays, maximum 2 consecutive wins when other players are waiting in line.
  • Zero Rage-Quitting: Never slam controllers, headsets, or keyboards. Treat gear with care.

Coach Mendez’s 3 Pillars (2026–27)

  1. Everyone Stays Eligible: Minimum 2.0 GPA with no Fs. Grade checks happen before every match day. The goal isn't to bench players—it's to catch struggles early so we can tutor, communicate, and recover together.
  2. More Diversity & Inclusion: Reaching out beyond gaming circles into art, theater, DATA Academy, and other clubs. Whether you're a competitive grinder or just want a safe hangout with AC at lunch, you belong here.
  3. Grow Beyond Playing: Real leadership and career roles for non-players: Game Captains, Stream Technicians, Video Editors, Shoutcasters, Graphic Designers, and Club Officers.

Privacy & Safety Standards

Cathedral City High School Esports complies with FERPA, COPPA, California SOPIPA, and PSUSD Board Policies (BP 0410, BP 5131.2, BP 6163.4). No private student records, grades, or personal identifying info are published on this site.
