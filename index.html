ROOM 315 CULTURE

Lab Etiquette & Norms

Our room is built on community, respect, and mutual support.

🤝 Community & Respect

  • Everyone Is Welcome: New players, seasoned veterans, or casual gamers—there is a place for everyone in Room 315.
  • Mutual Respect: Respect every person in the room. Treat teammates, opponents, and club members with dignity and kindness.
  • Everyone Plays: Share stations, rotate in, and make sure nobody is left sitting out. Support each other's growth.
  • Just Chilling is OK: You don't have to be a hardcore competitor. If you want to hang out, watch scrims, and chill with friends, that is 100% fine.

🛡️ Respect the Equipment

  • Care for Hardware: Treat all PCs, consoles, monitors, headsets, and controllers with pride. Zero throwing or slamming gear.
  • Clean Hands & Space: Keep hands clean before using peripherals. Use sanitizing wipes to wipe down controllers after use.
  • No Food Near Gear: Food and snacks must stay away from computer keyboards and Switch docks. Keep trash in bins.
  • Report Issues: If a cable, controller, or game crashes, notify Coach Mendez right away so we can fix it for everyone.
