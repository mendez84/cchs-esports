📋 Standards & Eligibility

Esports athletes represent Cathedral City High School and are subject to the same student-athlete requirements as CIF athletic programs.

🎓 Academic Standards

  • Must maintain a minimum 2.0 GPA with no failing grades (no Fs).
  • Bi-weekly grade checks are conducted to maintain active roster eligibility.
  • Academic tutoring is supported and encouraged prior to after-school match times.
  • Attendance compliance: unexcused school absences on match days disqualify play.

🤝 Code of Conduct & Sportsmanship

  • Strict zero-tolerance policy for toxicity, harassment, rage-quitting, or profanity in-game, in lab, or over voice chat.
  • Full adherence to the PSUSD Student Code of Conduct (BP 5131 / BP 5131.2).
  • Respect coaches, teammates, opponents, and match officials at all times. Win with humility, lose with character.

🔒 Student Privacy, Safety & District Compliance

Cathedral City High School and Palm Springs Unified School District are committed to student privacy, data protection, and online safety across all digital platforms.

FERPA & Student Records (34 CFR Part 99)

This website does not store, display, or transmit Personally Identifiable Information (PII), student ID numbers, academic grades, or confidential student education records. Roster mentions and recognitions are strictly limited to authorized gamer tags or require signed parental/guardian media consent forms on file with CCHS.

COPPA & California SOPIPA Compliance

This website employs zero third-party commercial advertising, zero behavioral marketing trackers, zero profiling, and zero tracking cookies. No personal information is collected, sold, or shared with commercial entities, fully adhering to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act and California's Student Online Personal Information Protection Act (Cal. Ed. Code § 49073.1).

District Technology & Safety Policies

Participation in CCHS Esports complies with PSUSD Board Policy 6163.4 (Student Use of Technology), BP 0410 (Nondiscrimination in District Programs), and BP 5131.2 (Bullying and Harassment Prevention). All team communications, practice sessions, and match activities are teacher-supervised in school facilities.

Secure District Forms

Any student interest surveys, roster submissions, or registration processes are administered through district-authenticated Google Workspace accounts (@psusd.us) and never via unverified third-party public forms.
